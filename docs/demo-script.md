# Walkthrough: use your own product and leads

1. Start SignalDesk and open Product & criteria. Define what you sell and who benefits; change one criterion to a product-specific implementation prerequisite. Save.
2. Import a small CSV of your own non-sensitive inquiries. Inspect the preview, import, and open a lead. Nothing has contacted a provider yet.
3. Connect your own key under AI connection. Select one lead, decide whether to fetch its website, review the transmission notice, and start qualification.
4. Inspect the result. Open a criterion's quote and its source snapshot. Explain why website evidence can support fit while readiness requires the prospect's statements.
5. If information is missing, show its score range and the suggested discovery question. Confirm or change the recommended route with a reason.
6. Export an account brief and a CSV. Change the product criteria and show that the earlier run is preserved and marked for requalification.

If no API key is available, use Assess manually and copy quotes from the inquiry. Label this as manual assessment; it does not demonstrate a live model response. The optional examples are useful for orientation, but reuse is demonstrated with a separate product and new inputs.
